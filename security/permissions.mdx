---
title: "What we can read"
description: "The permissions the CloudVerdict roles have in your account, and what they can never do."
---

You connect an account by deploying our CloudFormation template. It creates two IAM roles in your account:

| Role | Used for |
|---|---|
| Scanner role | Reading your configuration during scans. |
| Cross-account role | Verifying the connection only. |

## What the scanner role can do

- Read configuration through the AWS managed **SecurityAudit** policy, plus a small set of extra read-only configuration permissions our checks need.
- Nothing else. It has no permission to create, change or delete anything.

## What it can never do

The template explicitly **denies** reading the contents of your data, for example the objects in your S3 buckets. An explicit deny in IAM overrides any allow.

## Who can use the roles

Both roles trust only specific CloudVerdict platform roles, and only with the external ID unique to your connection. Nobody else can assume them, including other CloudVerdict customers.

## Optional checks

Some checks are off by default and need your opt-in on the account, for example looking for secret-looking values in Lambda function settings. When you turn one on, the values are checked in memory during the scan and never stored.

## Removing access

Delete the CloudFormation stack and the roles are gone. See [Connect your AWS account](/get-started/connect-aws#disconnecting-an-account).
