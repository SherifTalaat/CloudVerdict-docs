---
title: "What we can't see"
description: "The limits of what CloudVerdict checks, so you know where to look yourself."
---

- We read configuration, not the contents of your files, databases or messages.
- We only see the accounts and regions you connect.
- Some checks depend on logging you control; where it's off, we tell you rather than guess.
- Results are as fresh as your last scan.

When we can't see enough to decide whether a finding is a real risk, we mark it **Couldn't verify** and treat it at its full severity until we can. See [Verdicts](/concepts/verdicts).
