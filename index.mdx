A misconfiguration isn't always a risk. A server with an open port that nothing can reach, or a bucket that looks public but grants access to nobody, is worth fixing, but it isn't what you fix first.

CloudVerdict checks your AWS accounts against its security rules, then gives each finding a **verdict**: can someone use this today, or is it blocked by something else you have in place? Findings that chain together into a route an attacker could follow become **breach paths**. You get a short list of real risks, the paths that connect them, and a posture score you can track.

## Check our work

You don't have to take our word for it. Most findings come with read-only steps you can run in your own account to confirm them. See [Check our work](/concepts/check-our-work).
