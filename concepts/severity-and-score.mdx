## Severity

Every check has a severity: **Critical**, **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. The verdict can adjust it for your account:

- a finding that's **Not exposed today** counts one level lower;
- a finding that's **Not applicable here** doesn't count.

When we adjust it, the finding shows the original severity and why it changed. Remediation deadlines (SLAs) follow the adjusted severity.

## Posture score

Your posture score runs from 0 to 100. Each open finding adds weight by its adjusted severity:

| Severity | Weight |
|---|---|
| Critical | 15 |
| High | 8 |
| Medium | 3 |
| Low | 1 |

The score falls quickly for the first problems and more slowly after that, so fixing a few serious findings moves it the most.

- While any Critical finding is open, the score can't go above 70.
- Findings your organization has accepted as a risk still count, because accepting a risk doesn't remove it.
- Findings that are not applicable don't count.
- Your organization's score is the average of your accounts' scores, with production accounts weighing more than non-production ones.

## Framework scores

The framework pages (for example CIS) use a different measure: the share of controls that passed, out of those we could evaluate.
