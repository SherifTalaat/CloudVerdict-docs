You don't have to take our word for it. Most findings have a **Verify yourself** tab.

## Check it yourself (all plans)

Read-only console steps and CLI commands that confirm the finding in your own account. Where a check needs more than one command, you can copy it as a single script to paste into AWS CloudShell. The script only reads; it changes nothing.

## Prove it from outside (Enterprise)

Safe proof steps you run yourself, shown one at a time. You give your consent each time.

## Outside-in check (Enterprise)

With your permission, we test the exposure from the internet, from published IP addresses, and give you a signed result. Organization admins start it, and consent is asked every time.
