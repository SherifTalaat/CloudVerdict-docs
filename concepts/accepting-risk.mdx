---
title: "Accepting a risk"
description: "What happens when your organization decides not to fix a finding for now."
---

If your organization decides not to fix a finding for now, an admin can accept the risk with a reason and an end date: 7, 30 or 90 days (30 days at most for Critical findings).

While it's accepted, the finding:

- stays visible, marked **Accepted by your organization**;
- still counts in your posture score, in reports and in exports;
- stays on any breach path it's part of, marked **Accepted risk**.

When the acceptance ends, the finding reopens automatically and your organization's admins are notified.
