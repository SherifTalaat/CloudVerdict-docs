## When scans run

- **Automatically**, on your plan's schedule.
- **On demand**, when you select **Scan now** on an account card. On-demand scans count towards your plan's limit.

The first scan after you connect an account starts automatically and is free.

## What a scan does

A scan finds the resources in your account, checks each one against our rules, gives every finding a verdict, builds breach paths, and updates your posture score. [How CloudVerdict works](/concepts/how-it-works) explains each step.

When a scan finishes you get an email summary (you can change this in **Settings → Notifications**).

## Where to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Breach paths">
    Start with any path marked **Exploitable**. Fixing any one step breaks the path. See [Breach paths](/concepts/breach-paths).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Findings that are exposed now">
    On **Security findings**, filter by the verdict **Exposed now**. These are the findings someone could use today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The rest, by severity">
    Then work through Critical and High findings. Findings that are **Not exposed today** still matter: the protection that blocks them can change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Results are as fresh as your last scan. If you fix something, the next scan confirms it and closes the finding.
